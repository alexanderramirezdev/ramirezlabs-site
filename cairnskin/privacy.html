Privacy policy

Cairn Skin

Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version

Cairn Skin collects nothing. Your photos, area names, and notes stay in the app's storage on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and nowhere for your data to go.

What stays on your device

  • Photos you take in the app
  • The names you give your tracking areas, and any notes you add
  • Dates, comparison scores, and app settings

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics package, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK in the app. We cannot see your photos, and we have no way to identify you as a user.

Where photos are analysed

Image comparison runs on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No image is uploaded for processing.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. Exporting a PDF hands the file to iOS, and from there you decide where it goes — Files, Mail, a printer, another app. If your iPhone is set to back up to iCloud, your app data is included in that backup and is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Deleting your data

Delete a photo or a tracking area inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything at once. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Cairn Skin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above changes with it.
