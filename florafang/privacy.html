Privacy policy

FloraFang

Last updated 3 September 2026

The short version

FloraFang makes no network requests. Your photos, notes, and saved entries stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

  • Photos you capture in the app
  • Saved field log entries, including notes, assessments, and optional coarse locations
  • Anything you type into the exposure screen, which is never saved or sent anywhere
  • Conversations with the on-device Field Naturalist AI, which are processed entirely on your iPhone's Neural Engine
  • App settings

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Where identification happens

On your iPhone, using Apple's Vision framework, Core ML, and Apple Intelligence Foundation Models bundled with the app. No photograph is uploaded for analysis. The app contains a seam for a future remote identification tier; it is switched off, and while it is off FloraFang makes zero network calls.

The exposure screen

The details you enter there, meaning weight, timing, symptoms, and so on, exist only to be read aloud or copied to your clipboard for a call to poison control. They are held in memory while the screen is open and discarded when you close it. Nothing is saved to the field log, and nothing is transmitted. Tapping a hotline number hands off to the Phone app, which is a normal call, not something FloraFang can see.

Location

FloraFang requests optional location permission to record coarse coordinates (rounded to two decimal places, roughly one kilometre) and a reverse-geocoded city or region name when you save an entry. This is used solely on-device to help narrow down species geographic ranges and organize your field log. Precise street-level coordinates are never recorded, location is never tracked in the background, and your location data is never transmitted to us or any third party. If you choose to export your field log, the export archive you create includes these coarse coordinates, and where that file goes is entirely up to you.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics package, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK. We have no way to identify you as a user and no way to see anything you photograph.

Camera

FloraFang needs camera access to identify anything. The camera feed is used for the viewfinder and for the photo you choose to capture. It is not recorded or transmitted.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the field log, or delete the app to remove everything at once. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

FloraFang is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If the remote tier is ever switched on

Turning it on would mean photographs leaving your device, which would be a material change. It would be opt‑in, it would be visible in the app, and this policy would be rewritten before it shipped.
