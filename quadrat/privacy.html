Privacy policy

Quadrat

Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version

Quadrat makes no network requests. Your photos, notes, and saved entries stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

  • Photos you capture in the app
  • Saved field log entries, including the notes you write and the assessment the app gave at the time
  • App settings

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Where identification happens

On your iPhone, using Apple's Vision framework and an on-device model. No photograph is uploaded for analysis. The app contains a seam for a future remote identification tier; it is switched off, and while it is off Quadrat makes zero network calls.

Location

Quadrat does not request or record your location. It does not ask for location permission.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics package, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK. We have no way to identify you as a user and no way to see anything you photograph.

Camera

Quadrat needs camera access to identify anything. The camera feed is used for the viewfinder and for the photo you choose to capture. It is not recorded or transmitted.

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the field log, or delete the app to remove everything at once. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Quadrat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

If the remote tier is ever switched on

Turning it on would mean photographs leaving your device, which would be a material change. It would be opt‑in, it would be visible in the app, and this policy would be rewritten before it shipped.
