Support

Quadrat

Email support@ramirezlabs.app and a person will reply. Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and if a specific scan went wrong, roughly what you were pointing at.

Why won't it name the species?

Usually because it can't, and saying so is the point. The offline classifier recognises broad categories — spider, beetle, flower — not individual species. When Quadrat isn't confident enough to be useful, it tells you that instead of picking the highest-scoring guess and presenting it as fact.

Then what am I supposed to do with a refusal?

A refusal comes with what to do next rather than a dead end — how to get a better photo, or where to take the question. It is a result, not an error.

How do I get a better identification?

Fill the square frame with the subject and get close. Cropping tightly to the organism is the single biggest thing you control — a spider on a wall can be two percent of the picture, and at that size the app is mostly looking at stucco. Recent iPhones switch to the macro lens automatically when you get close enough.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Identification runs entirely on your iPhone and the app makes no network requests at all.

Why does it seem more cautious about dangerous things?

Deliberately. Wrongly flagging a harmless spider costs you a moment of concern. Wrongly calling a dangerous one harmless costs a great deal more. So a hazard warning has a lower bar to appear than an all-clear does, and the app breaks ties toward caution.

Where are my saved scans?

In the field log, along with the note you added and exactly what the app told you at the time — not what it would say today. Saving is manual, so a scan you didn't save isn't there.

Quadrat is not a safety authority and not medical advice. Do not decide whether something is safe to touch, eat, or approach based on this app. If you have been bitten or stung and feel unwell, contact emergency services or Poison Control at 1‑800‑222‑1222 (US) right away. Do not wait to identify anything first.
