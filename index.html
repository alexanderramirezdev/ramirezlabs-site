Cairn Skin

A photo journal for your skin. Set up tracking areas for specific spots on your body, photograph them over time with a ghost overlay that lines up each new shot with the last, and see how much has changed. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no upload, no server.

iOS Vision / Core ML In development

Quadrat

Identifies plants, animals, and fungi from your camera, and tells you when it can't. Most apps in this category will name a species at 40% confidence. That is fine for a wildflower and dangerous for a spider in your child's room, so Quadrat treats "I can't tell you, and here's what to do instead" as a real answer rather than a failure. Identification runs offline; the app makes no network calls.

ClearChart

A spatial workspace for clinical documentation. Patient vitals, labs, and medications sit in panels around the room instead of on a screen the clinician has to turn away to read, and notes are captured by voice while the clinician keeps their hands and attention on the patient. Currently a research prototype running on placeholder records.

macOS Foundation Models Research

B.E.T.H.

Basic Everyday Task Helper. A voice assistant that runs entirely on the machine in front of you, using Apple's on-device language model rather than a cloud API. It doubles as the test bench for ongoing doctoral research into how reliably a small local model can turn plain speech into concrete actions.
